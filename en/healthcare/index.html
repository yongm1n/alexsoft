Frequently asked questions

Before we define the scope.

What kinds of EMR and hospital-system integration do you handle?+

We first review the required workflows, such as care, appointments, registration, testing and billing, along with the interfaces available in the existing systems. After defining the feasible integration scope and data, we design the APIs, messages and operating procedures.

Can you also review EMR certification requirements?+

During architectural design, we review how certification requirements affect data structures, functional boundaries and operating procedures. We do not guarantee certification; applicable criteria and the assessment scope are checked against current materials at the start of the engagement.

Is the proposal tied to a particular EMR vendor?+

No. Our work does not depend on selling a particular vendor's product. We examine the actual interfaces and constraints of your current systems, then design independently of vendors while considering standards and the ability to replace components.

Will you build an EMR product for us?+

No. We do not take on the development of an entire new EMR product. We handle integration with existing systems, healthcare information architecture, design for certification requirements and the development needed for that scope.

How do you handle patient and healthcare-organization data?+

We request only what is necessary for the assessment and follow agreed transfer methods and retention periods. Initial reviews use de-identified structures and sample data. If actual personal data is needed, we first agree on access, processing and disposal rules.
