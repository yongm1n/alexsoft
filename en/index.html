Core expertise

Structure first, then the build — inside the constraints that cannot move.

Laws, certification and systems already in production are treated as design inputs from day one, not as a checklist at the end. Four areas where that discipline has been exercised on real systems.

How an engagement runs

From judgement to delivery, one unbroken line.

Structure is judged before anything is built, and whoever designs it builds it. Nothing is handed over between stages, so nothing is lost on the way. Fees are quoted in writing once the scope is confirmed.

← Swipe to see the whole flow →

Fit call
30 minutes, free. A conversation to see whether it is the right fit. No documents are produced.
Assessment
Half a day to one day of architecture assessment: a risk map of the current structure and the three to five decisions that have to be made. Paid in advance and fully credited toward a follow-on engagement.
Design
Design and technical due diligence, two to six weeks: regulatory-driven design, legacy modernization road maps, vendor and technology selection, due diligence for investment and M&A. 50% due at kickoff.
Build
ALEXSOFT implements what it designed. Integration, back-office and MVP-scale systems directly; larger systems with design oversight and vetted partners. Two concurrent builds at most. Milestone billing.
Advisory
A fixed number of days per month as the standing architect: design reviews, technical decisions, questions from the development team. Paid monthly in advance.
