Products and projects

Proof that the designer carries the work through to completion.

Products and services conceived, built and made available by ALEXSOFT: an open-source engine mapping impact from code to database columns, a tool showing team goals in five views, a tool converting EMR and LIS data to Korean standards, and web services exploring Busan and Geoje through terrain, statistics and archive photographs. The project pages show their screens and architecture in detail.

Cascade — impact maps from code to columns

A static-analysis engine that answers “Which SQL statements, methods, APIs and screens are affected if this column changes?” using source code alone. The viewer and MCP server provide the same answers. Published on GitHub under Apache-2.0.

See Cascade’s screens and architecture →

Mokpyo — team goals in five views

A team goal and OKR tool with card, table, board, timeline and dashboard views over the same goal data. Sub-goal progress rolls up automatically, with automation for deadlines and status changes running behind the scenes.

See Mokpyo’s screens and architecture →

ALEXSOFT Bridge — healthcare data in Korean standard formats

An interoperability tool that converts data from hospital and clinic EMR and LIS systems to Korea’s KR Core 2.0.0 format, reporting every unconverted record with its reason.

See Bridge’s screens and architecture →

Busan & Geoje in View — terrain, statistics and archives on a map

A non-profit toy project bringing the terrain of Busan and Geoje, scattered public statistics and photographs together in one place to explore. Both services are in Korean.

About

More than 25 years in the field inform every decision.

ALEXSOFT founder Yongmin Lee served as development project lead from 2020 to 2022 on the Philippine BIR e-invoice project (KOICA, 2018–2022), designing document and exchange security standards and leading development. The system has remained in operation since its 2022 launch. He then worked on the first cloud product among EMR-certified clinic EMRs from 2022 to 2026, starting as development lead in 2022 and heading development and product planning and design from 2023 through 2026. His work spans more than 25 years of development, architecture and project management across shipbuilding and marine engineering, the public sector and healthcare.
