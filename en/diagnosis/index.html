When this helps

Some decisions call for an architecture review first.

We focus on decisions that are hard to reverse, across industries and tools. If any of the following applies, there is a reason to examine the architecture before moving into design.

Regulatory or certification requirements directly shape the design.

Adding these requirements later can mean redesigning the architecture. We treat them as constraints from the start.

The system must connect to existing core systems, EMRs or ERPs.

We define the data and responsibility boundaries at integration points before design begins.

You need to decide whether to build, buy or integrate.

We compare all three options against the same criteria and document the reasoning behind the choice.

You have a development team, but no one owns the architecture.

We fill that architectural decision-making role for as long as it is needed.

Engagement stages

Go as deep as the work requires, one stage at a time.

You do not need to commit to everything at once. Start with a 20-minute call, then move through assessment, design, development and ongoing advice. You can stop at any stage.

Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

What happens during the 20-minute fit check?+

We hear about the problem you want to solve, the constraints, your existing systems and your team, then assess whether the work is a good fit. We give our assessment verbally; no analysis documents or designs are provided.

How does an assessment session differ from design work?+

The assessment session takes half a day to one day to identify risks in the current architecture and decisions that need to be made. Design takes two to six weeks to define the target architecture and document it so that a development team can start work directly.

What happens to the assessment fee if we continue?+

The assessment fee is paid in advance. If you proceed to a design engagement or monthly advisory retainer, it is credited in full against the contract amount. You are also welcome to stop after the assessment.

Why are prices not listed on the website?+

The effort can vary greatly with system size, constraints and the scope of involvement, even for work with the same name. We narrow the scope during the 20-minute fit check, then quote for that scope.

What kinds of systems are a good fit?+

Systems whose design is directly shaped by regulation or certification, and systems that must integrate with core enterprise systems. This includes public-sector and healthcare systems with predefined requirements, or projects that need a new architecture alongside existing systems.

Can you also handle development?+

Yes. We directly build the systems we design. To maintain quality, we limit concurrent development projects to two.

How does the monthly advisory retainer work?+

We agree on a fixed number of days each month and participate as your ongoing architect for that time. Design reviews, technical decisions and development-team questions are covered within those days, with payment made monthly in advance.

How do you handle company materials?+

We request only what is necessary for the assessment and follow agreed transfer methods and retention periods. If sensitive materials are involved, we agree on confidentiality arrangements before starting.
