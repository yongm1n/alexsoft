Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

How is the quote determined?+

We review the page count, content readiness, required features and interactions, then propose a scope and quote. You can start with the experience you need most now, without including every feature from the outset.

Is the website automatically generated from a template?+

No. Information architecture, art direction, design, development and quality review are decided and carried out directly to suit the project's purpose.

Can we start before the copy and images are ready?+

We can agree on a scope for organizing the basic copy. Professional copywriting, brand photography and large-scale image production are quoted separately if needed.

Can you work with Imweb or WordPress?+

We choose the approach best suited to how you will operate the site and the features you need. We assess design quality, ease of operation and long-term cost together, rather than starting with a particular tool.

Can we make changes ourselves after launch?+

If content will change frequently, we include a CMS or content-management approach in the scope before starting. For a static site, you own the code and deployment accounts, and can request changes separately when needed.
