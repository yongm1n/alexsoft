e-Invoicing through a Korean lens

What Korea calls electronic tax invoicing is known internationally as e-invoicing.

In Korea, electronic tax-invoice issuance and transmission and VAN-based sales-data reporting to the National Tax Service have generally been treated as separate tasks. In several countries, e-invoicing handles document exchange and sales reporting within one set of specifications and transmission architecture.

INVOICE

Electronic tax invoices

Parties to a transaction issue structured tax invoices and transmit them to recipients and tax authorities.

SALES DATA

VAN tax-authority reporting

Payment and sales data are collected and reported in the structure required by the National Tax Service.

E-INVOICING

Document exchange and reporting

One system standardizes invoice, transaction and payment data for validation, transmission, reporting and tracing.

This is why the Philippine system is officially called the Electronic Invoicing and Sales Reporting System. It handles both the exchange of electronic invoices and the sales-data reporting required by the tax authority.

National system experience

From document standards to government handover.

ALEXSOFT founder Yongmin Lee participated as development PL from 2020 to 2022 in the Philippine Bureau of Internal Revenue e-invoice system project (KOICA, 2018–2022). He designed the development architecture, e-invoice document standards and document-exchange security standards, and led development. The system went live in 2022 and remains in operation.

Frequently asked questions

Two common questions.

How does e-invoicing differ from Korean electronic tax invoices?+

In Korea, issuing and transmitting tax invoices and sending sales data to the National Tax Service through VANs have generally been treated as separate flows. In several countries, e-invoicing brings these together in one electronic document exchange and reporting framework.

What does the Philippine BIR EIS experience contribute?+

It is experience designing document standards for many businesses, exchange security and operational handover together at national scale. Founder Yongmin Lee participated as development PL in 2020–2022 during the initial 2018–2022 project. This is separate from the 2025–2026 second-phase follow-up support carried out by another provider.

Where this experience applies

We apply this experience to architectural decisions. We do not offer country-specific compliance packages.
